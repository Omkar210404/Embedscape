    An interactive map of how AI sees words

    Every dot is a word. Close dots have similar meanings.

    1. Words become numbers. To a computer, each word is a list of numbers, called an embedding. Here every word has 50 of them.
    2. Similar use, similar numbers. The numbers were learned from billions of sentences, so words used in similar ways get similar numbers.
    3. They become a globe. We laid the words out on a globe so that related words sit near each other, forming continents of meaning you can spin and zoom into.

    This is the same idea that lets modern AI search find things by meaning instead of exact keywords.
